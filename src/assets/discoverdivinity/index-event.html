ABOUT THE EVENT

Find inner peace in a busy world

Your life is meant to be one of joy and cheer. A human being alone has the potential to attain everlasting happiness. However, humans today are unaware of their inner design, much less use it to live in harmony and happiness.

Seize this opportunity to rediscover yourself. Fortify your personality to become a beacon of joy and happiness. Through a clear and practical presentation, Sushilaji reveals the timeless wisdom in Chapter 10 of the Bhagavad Gita. The Gita’s universal truths offer powerful techniques to achieve lasting peace and prosperity in life.

WHY GITA

Manual for Life

The Bhagavad Gita provides a practical guide for mankind to live a life of peace and prosperity. Its timeless truths are applicable in any period of time to every human being. The knowledge of the Gita helps one develop a deeper understanding of oneself and relate correctly with the world.

A step towards discovering yourself.

ABOUT THE SPEAKER

Sushilaji

Sushilaji has dedicated over three decades to the full-time study, research and propagation of Vedanta – the ancient philosophy of India. Her logical and systematic presentation of the subject in contemporary thought and language has been widely appreciated. Sushilaji has also addressed educational and corporate institutions worldwide on the practical application of Vedanta.

TRADITION

Gita Yajna

For over five decades, Swami A. Parthasarathy conducted the Gita Gnana Yajna, a public lecture series on the Bhagavad Gita, across India and the globe. The effort was to make the invaluable wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita accessible to all humanity. In Visakhapatnam, the tradition continues through Sushilaji, senior disciple of Swamiji.
