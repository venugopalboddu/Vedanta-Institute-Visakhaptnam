Vedanta Institute Visakhapatnam

The organization is a non-profit public charitable trust registered in Andhra Pradesh, founded under the guidance of A. Parthasarathy. Its primary purpose is to harmonize the physical, emotional, intellectual, and spiritual aspects of human beings, without discrimination based on color, race, or religion. The trust places a special emphasis on inspiring young minds to pursue ideals beyond selfish interests, ultimately guiding them towards the goal of Self-Realization.

To spread the knowledge of life and living for the betterment of society, the trust organizes annual public discourses on Vedanta. These discourses are conducted by Swami Parthasarathy and Sushilaji, a senior disciple of A. Parthasarathy, and attract a diverse audience. The trust's objectives are supported through donations, which are exempt from income tax under section 80G of the Income Tax Act.
