S.V. Satyanarayana was invited to give a seminar titled "The Key to Success" on July 25, 2024. The event, organized by the Andhra University Alumni Association (AAA), featured Satyanarayana, a distinguished former student of Andhra University. He had a notable corporate career in both India and the UK before dedicating himself to the in-depth study and research of Vedanta Philosophy under the guidance of his spiritual guru, A. Parthasarathy, spending five years at the Vedanta Academy.

Satyanarayana is now a resource at the Vedanta Institute Visakhapatnam, a charitable trust focused on the holistic development of individuals to achieve peace and prosperity. Since 2006, he has been residing in Visakhapatnam, conducting workshops on various management topics for corporates, personality development sessions for college and school students, as well as public discourses and study classes on the Bhagavad Gita and other scriptural texts.
