What is Vedanta?

Vedanta is derived from two words: Veda, knowledge, and Anta, end. Vedanta means the end of knowledge. This ancient philosophy presents the eternal principles of life and living. It programmes a life of mental peace combined with dynamic action in the world. It equips one with clarity of intellect to deal with the challenges of life. Above all, its philosophy leads one to the ultimate goal of Self-Realisation.

A. Parthasarathy
